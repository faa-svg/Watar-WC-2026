🏟️ Group Stage

BONUS
Upset Bonus — Odds-Based
If you predicted the underdog and they won, you earn extra points based on real bookmaker odds. The bigger the upset, the bigger the bonus. Works for group stage AND all knockout rounds.
Predicting Saudi Arabia beats Argentina = massive bonus. Predicting France beats Haiti = small bonus.
